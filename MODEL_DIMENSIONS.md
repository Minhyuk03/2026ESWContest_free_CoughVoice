# Raspberry Pi 5 microphone holder dimensions

- Base footprint: 85 × 56 mm (Raspberry Pi 5 official PCB outline)
- Base thickness: 3 mm
- Microphone opening: Ø20 mm
- Holder wall: 3 mm (Ø26 mm outside)
- Holder depth: 12 mm
- Holder center: 50 mm above the top surface of the base
- Overall height: 66 mm
- Center support: 12 × 12 mm
- Holder type: open-top circular snap cradle

The 20 mm request is interpreted as the internal microphone opening diameter.
